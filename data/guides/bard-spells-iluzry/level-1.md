# 1st Level Spells

## Control

* [**Grease**](https://aonprd.com/SpellDisplay.aspx?ItemName=grease)**:** The OG control spell. Choose a 10 ft square. Everyone in that square make a reflex save (Which monsters tend to be meh at) or fall prone, making them easy cleanup for your melee team. You’ll find...other uses for it in the future, but trust me when i say this is a go-to for a reason.
* **✰[Deja Vu](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/magic/all-spells/d/deja-vu/)✰:** Okay no save, your opponent has to repeat whatever actions it took in the last round, for two rounds, and if they can’t, they are confused. Making enemies more predictable is fantastic, or forcing enemies to act in a way that keeps you in the advantage, is awesome. This is sort of an insane first level control spell.

## Buff

* [**Tears to Wine**](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/magic/all-spells/t/tears-to-wine/): Give your team massive bonuses to skills for a long time! Very much benefits from a strong caster level, and well, there are a lot of INT and WIS checks we care about too.
* [**Expeditious Retreat**](https://aonprd.com/SpellDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Expeditious%20Retreat)**:** Aka, GAS GAS GAS the spell. Just gives you a lot of free movement speed, and that is always helpful.
* [**Liberating Command**](https://aonprd.com/SpellDisplay.aspx?ItemName=liberating%20command) **(also Protection):** Lesser Freedom of movement! Give someone who is grappled but can still move their body double your CL as a bonus to escape artist checks and they can make one right then and there! Immediate action makes it divine and there will always be SOMEBODY who gets yoinked.

## Debuff

* [**Ear-Piercing Scream**](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/magic/all-spells/e/ear-piercing-scream/) **(also Blast):** REEEEEEEEEEEEEEEE. You scream and the target takes some meh sonic damage (still very rarely resisted) and has to make a fort save or be DAZED which is...another ouchy ouch condition. Anything we can do to make the enemy lose actions, we take.
* [**Hideous Laughter**](https://aonprd.com/SpellDisplay.aspx?ItemName=hideous%20laughter)**:** Will save, or your opponent can take no actions. They aren’t helpless! But...they can’t do shit. Which is hilarious. They can try to make a new save as a full round action, but that still wastes their time, and wasting their time is the name of the game for debuffs.

## Summons

* [**Animate Rope**](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/magic/all-spells/a/animate-rope/)**:** Funny note, doesn’t HAVE to be a rope, just rope like. Curtains, Chains...whatever you have around can function. It’s a versatile little partner too, being able to entangle foes one minute, or make triplines to stop pursuers, or help you climb the castle walls! Use with mage hand to have some fun little options.
* [**Summon Minor Monster**](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/magic/all-spells/s/summon-minor-monster/)**:** So this spell is fun because the only limitations are 1.) you can’t summon it in an environment that cannot support it and 2.) it has to be tiny or smaller animals...which does not necessarily indicate their power :3 Throw out a skunk for debuffs, some Owls or Hawks for airborne dpr, bats for detecting invisible creatures, or just some other critter to scratch their face off. You’ve got options!!!!

## Utility

* [**Auditory Hallucination**](https://aonprd.com/SpellDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Auditory%20Hallucination)**:** Aka GREATER ghost sounds. How is this better than the at will cantrip? Well a few things. First of all, better range and no sound upper limit...but it also is sneakier, as you can choose which creatures to effect by it. This can also work on your own team as a sort of...psuedo telepathy or with collaborators as a pseudo tongues.. But most importantly, you don’t actually have to KNOW what the thing you want ACTUALLY sounds like. You just have to have an idea of it. Never spoken the guards language? It’s fine! Don’t know what that monster sounds like? It’s cool. So long as you have the idea, it flies! Great in a creative players hands.
* **✰[Silent Image](https://www.aonprd.com/SpellDisplay.aspx?ItemName=silent%20image)✰ (Also Control):** This spell accompanied by ghost sounds ties with mount and alter summoned monster for crazy versatility. I’m serious! You can make this look like anything and with silent image, it can sounds like anything, and for most people, if it looks like a duck and sounds like a duck, its a duck, and you can abuse that. Moreover, unlike the other combination, this one is dirt cheap...both of them spells are good on their own! Just requires a creative mind. *Remember, creatures can only disbelieve your illusion if they directly interact with it. They don’t get a save from just seeing it unless it says so.*
* [**Charm Person**](https://www.aonprd.com/SpellDisplay.aspx?ItemName=charm%20person)**:** Make someone your best friend! This lasts for ONE HOUR PER LEVEL so if you get someone, you can REALLY get them, and to convince them to do something for you, you need to make a charisma check….OH HEY WE ARE GOOD AT THOSE!!! The magic of friendship should never be underestimated.
* [**Solid Note**](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/magic/all-spells/s/solid-note/) **(also Summon)**: Summon lesser immovable rod. It only has a strength of 10+CL but thats still enough to hold you for a lot of shenanigans. Use them as jump platforms, barricades, anchors and much much more! Involves a bit of creativity, but I think a spell as unique as this deserves some time in the sun.

## Protection

* [**Vanish**](https://www.aonprd.com/SpellDisplay.aspx?ItemName=vanish) **(also Utility):** So at level 1 this isn’t great, because you basically cast it on yourself and then move and then it goes away...but it gets better and better. You’ll want to trade it out for its older brother, invisibility, when you get the chance, but for now, being able to vanish and mess with people indirectly is fantastic.
* **✰[Saving Finale](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/magic/all-spells/s/saving-finale/)✰:** Are you, the bard, planning on using a lot of helpful bardic performances? Yeah? Then this is a godsend. An immediate action re-roll at the cost of a spell slot and your performance ending early is a super easy trade and will be useful from 1-20. This will save SEVERAL lives.

## 1st Lvl Alt Sources

* [**Moment of Greatness**](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/magic/all-spells/m/moment-of-greatness/) **(wand):** You have A LOT of morale bonuses to throw around, and being able to double them, even for one action will always be useful for you. Doesn’t really care about caster level and makes for a cheap purchase. Definitely worth buying.
* [**Feather Fall**](https://aonprd.com/SpellDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Feather%20fall) **(wand / [snapleaf](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/magic-items/wondrous-items/r-z/snapleaf/) / [ring of featherfall](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/magic-items/rings/ring-of-feather-falling/)):** When you need it? You need it. Otherwise, we can live without it. If someone else has this prepared you can go without.
* [**Tap Inner Beauty**](https://aonprd.com/SpellDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Tap%20Inner%20Beauty) **(scroll/wand):** Small insight bonus to charisma checks is small but insightful! Since it doesn’t scale, works better on an item.
* [**Disguise Self**](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/magic/all-spells/d/disguise-self/) **([Hat of Disguise](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/magic-items/wondrous-items/h-l/hat-of-disguise/)):** Being able to be other people is great! But why burn a spell known when you can instead, just get this awesome fashion accessory and take the chameleon scales cantrip.
* [**Comprehend Languages**](https://aonprd.com/SpellDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Comprehend%20Languages) **(scroll):** Incredibly helpful given the nature of languages in PF1e BUTTTT not something we will be dealing with every day.
* [**Unseen Servant**](https://aonprd.com/SpellDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Unseen%20Servant) **(wand):** Nice helpful little utility summon that can do some basic housework for you. Nothing crazy, but it gets the job done.
* [**Lucky Number**](https://aonprd.com/SpellDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Lucky%20Number) **(wand):** Getting a once a day reroll can be really good but its too random for a spell slot.
* [**Ears of the City**](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/magic/all-spells/e/ears-of-the-city/) **(scroll/versatile spontaneity/arrowsongs’ Lament)**: Gathering information is fun, and knowing things is always useful. This is great if your DM makes you do this often, or if want to gather information from the shadows, but you likely won’t have to do that all of the time. That said, it makes for a great establishing ability for the setting, so I’d definitely have it in your back pocket at least.
* [**Heightened Awareness**](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/magic/all-spells/h/heightened-awareness/) **(wand):** +2 to perception checks and knowledge checks for 10 minutes per level, annnd can be dispelled for a +4 to initiative.
* [**Feather Step**](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/magic/all-spells/f/feather-step/) **(Scroll):** Heeyy ignore difficult terrain. This isn’t a problem you will deal with everyday, but when you do, its nice to have a solution for it.
* [**Cure Light Wounds**](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/magic/all-spells/c/cure-light-wounds/) **(wand):** It’s a staple and it pays to have some sort of healing option early on.
