# 2nd Level Spells

## Control

* **✰[Haunting Mists](https://aonprd.com/SpellDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Haunting%20Mists)✰** **(Also Debuff)**: SO this can potentially be devastating as a control spell, as since it is an illusion, you can give you team signals to KNOW that its fake, thus having your teammates being able to see through the mists and not your enemies. Remember, the will save is against the wisdom damage, not against the fog itself, which is once again, an illusion, meaning they have to interact with it in a meaningful way to disbelieve. Can’t just walk into and be like “well golly gee this ain’t fog.” (Also it is SR no which is VERY important)

## Buff

* [**Heroism**](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/magic/all-spells/h/heroism/)**:** It isn’t nearly as potent as haste but getting +2 to attack and saves for 10 minutes per level is an insanely long bonus, which makes it great for pre-buffing as the morale bonus stacks with basically everything else.
* [**Allegro**](https://www.aonprd.com/SpellDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Allegro)**:** Haste so long as you keep performing! Do you like haste? Of course you do. Obviously, if you are planning on having Blazing Rondo be your main performance, then you don’t need this but for any other bard you wants to have one of the best buffs in the game early for just doing what they were going to do anyway…here ya go!
* **✰[Gallant Inspiration](https://aonprd.com/SpellDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Gallant%20Inspiration)✰:** Immediate action buff to an attack or skill check. Need to convince the king and you are just below the threshold? GALLANT INSPIRATION. JUST miss that dragon? Gallant inspiration. Now it doesnt stack with inspire courage, but it does stack with basically EVERYTHING ELSE and is again…an immediate action. You WILLLLL find a use for it and you DEFINITELY should have it.
* [**Tactical Acumen**](https://aonprd.com/SpellDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Tactical%20Acumen)**:** Hey wide range buff for just doing tacticial things that your party should be doing. It’s not huge, but bard is much more about getting a lot of little numbers to add together, than any single explosive buff.
* [**Contagious Zeal**](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/magic/all-spells/c/contagious-zeal/)**:** Lesser good hope. Wide range buff that stacks with inspire courage and like 90% of your performances, is a morale bonus that can be boosted and naturally spreads itself. This should probably be one of the first buffs you cast in combat just to get the most use out of it but seriously, this should not be underestimated. Trade out for good hope later on.

## Debuff

* [**Hold Person**](https://aonprd.com/SpellDisplay.aspx?ItemName=hold%20person)**:** Will save or be paralysed which does just cause someone to lose more times than not.
* [**Cacophonous Call**](https://aonprd.com/SpellDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Cacophonous%20Call): Will save or nauseated hurts as it will effectively just remove someone from the fight. ITs still a will save with SR but honestly? Aren’t they all?
* [**Blindness/Deafness**](https://www.aonprd.com/SpellDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Blindness/Deafness)**:** Look at a person and say “fuck you in particular”. That person makes a fort save or is blinded or deafened….forever I guess lmao. This is an incredibly rude thing to do...but like ya know...it fucking works. Blind the archer, deafen the caster, make everyone’s day hell and remind them why the bard is to be feared. Fun note...it’s a verbal only spell, meaning you can use it without your hands :D
* [**Blistering Invective**](https://aonprd.com/SpellDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Blistering%20Invective) **(also Blast):** So yes it does damage. We are not here for the damage. We are here for what is effectively dazzling display as a standard action…as a spell. You can have a REALLY high intimidate score and there is no SR or save against that, you just get to roll it and see how ruined they are. Pairs EXCELLENTLY with Improved Dirge of Doom, where you can cast this spell, make a bunch of people shaken and then immediately frighten them, no save!
* [**Glitterdust**](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/magic/all-spells/g/glitterdust/)**:** This spell was made by gays, for gays...but we’ll share with everyone else. GLITTER BLAST!!!! So this also blinds people, but at least it’s not forever, and completely removes any chance they had at stealthing or invisibility ever. Also remember, this effects multiple people in a 10 foot spread. Time to ruin that platoon!
* [**Mortal Terror**](https://aonprd.com/SpellDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Mortal%20Terror)**:** Okay this can make fear coniditions...basically skyrocket, but they HAVE to fail that first will save. If they do this spell can get NASTY, as every time they take damage, they have to make another save or their fear gets WORSE. Shakened is one thing. Frightened, and Panicked are a completely different beast.
* **✰[Mental Block](https://aonprd.com/SpellDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Mental%20block)✰:** A divination spell that…straight up just takes away someone’s spells, supernatural abilities, AND EXTRAORDINARY ABILITIES!!!! WOWZA!!!! This is a straight up busted spell that is only balanced by its low save and the fact that its mind affecting. STILL if you can land this on some high level enemies? They ARE DONE.

## Summons

* [**Whip of Spiders**](https://docs.google.com/document/u/0/d/1zUWy_N76REjznrWlOzsDYy6Akast4bxS8z_RkLZgegQ/edit)  **(also Blast/Debuff):** You get a neat weapon that attacks at touch ac, does poison damage and possibly nausates your enemy! Lets anyone be a pseudo martial

## Utility

* [**Suggestion**](https://www.aonprd.com/SpellDisplay.aspx?ItemName=suggestion): Have a new friend help you out for hours per level! Now, your saves aren’t the best for this…but what kinda bard doesn’t play with the hearts of men and gods just a little?
* **✰[Silence](https://aonprd.com/SpellDisplay.aspx?ItemName=silence)✰** **(Also Debuff):** So just remember that all of YOUR Abilities are usually based on sound and this is an amazing way to shut down casters. Throw a rock at them and have them silenced with no save, or put it on one of your arrows and shoot someone in the chest. Honestly, the uses for this spell cannot be understated, especially in combination with invisibility!!!

## Protection

* **✰[Mirror Image](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/magic/all-spells/m/mirror-image/)✰:** THE BEST Defensive spell at this level, and frankly for the majority of the game. You will always cast this. I’m not joking. This? This should be the first mitigation spell you pick up because it’ll NEVER be unhelpful. Even if there is 1 image, that is basically a 50% miss chance. If there are 3? 4? 8?????? This spell makes you much much harder to bring down. THE BARD USED DOUBLE TEAM. THEIR EVASION ROSE!!!!
* **✰[Invisibility](https://aonprd.com/SpellDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Invisibility)✰ (also Utility):** HAHAHA ooooh this demon of a spell. Another classic from the arcane school? Why? Because it last minutes per level and the ONLY thing that breaks it is direct damage. So long as you can manage to NOT blast? Buff? Summoning? Debuffing (without attack rolls) All fair game. Also your rogues will love you and give you some nice loot.
