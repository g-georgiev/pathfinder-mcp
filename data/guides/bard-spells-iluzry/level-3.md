# 3rd Level Spells

## Control

* [**Confusion**](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/magic/all-spells/c/confusion/) **(also Debuff):** Alright, everyone in this 15ft circle? Will save (with SR) or have a 75% chance of being aBSOLUTELY useless this round….which can be turned into a 100% chance if you are a caster who gets bapped and must now attempt to bap a fighter. Have. Fucking. Fun.

## Buff

* **✰[Good Hope](https://aonprd.com/SpellDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Good%20Hope)✰:** Again, doesn’t seem like much but this is a wide range morale buff that you can give you your entire team that they will all love. It lasts a long time, boosts saves and attack rolls and is all around a fucking fantastic spell that you will love basically forever. Don’t be afraid to cast this with Encouraging spell and possibly even extreme mood swings in order to becomes a stat demon, and is objectively better than the flagbearer feat even with the banner of ancient kings.
* [**Haste**](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/magic/all-spells/h/haste/): *+1 to attack rolls* (oh thats neat i guess…), *+1 dodge bonus to ac* (oh i mean meh but like nice), *+1 to reflex saves* (oh okay cool), *+30 to ALL MOVEMENT SPEED* (Jesus fuck oka-) *WHEN MAKING A FULL ATTACK YOU CAN MAKE AN **EXTRA ATTACK** AT **FULL BAB BONUS.*** At this level, the fighter has 2 attacks in a full attack...this gives him a third. That’s a potential 50% increase in DPR...and you can drop this on your entire team. **This is THE combat buff**, and while the wizard CAN have it, you SHOULD have it because you will ALWAYS USE IT, from now until level 20.

## Debuff

* [**Sculpt Sound**](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/magic/all-spells/s/sculpt-sound/): True Ghost sounds. You can make anyone say whatever you want them to say, have anything sound like anything or like nothing and be a whole bastard about it. I THINK it gets a will save.
* [**Slow**](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/magic/all-spells/s/slow/)**:** Alright….entire enemy team? Save or be staggered for the entirety of combat by fuckoff time magic. Also have your movement HALVED. This? This is one of the best debuffs in the game.
* [**Fear**](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/magic/all-spells/f/fear/)**:** Save (w/ SR) or be removed from combat. I’m serious. It’s a emotion, mind effecting, fear effect, so there is a lot that is flat out immune but what isn’t...well...if they fail their save they are just kinda OUTIE. Ah I love the save or lose level. It’s so much fun.: **[Mydriatic Spontaneity](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/magic/all-spells/m/mydriatic-spontaneity/):** Aka, Nauseating Blindness, as you basically smack them with the Seizure Beam MKII, causing one person to save or be nauseated. This is really just a fun one.:

## Summons

* [**Phantom Steed**](https://www.aonprd.com/SpellDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Phantom%20Steed)**:** Summon scaling super horse! I told you I could get you a better mount. As you level up, your (incredibly fast) mount of choice gets even more movement options, all the way to being able to fly under its own power while you ride to victory at the speed of sound.
* [**Mad Monkeys**](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/magic/all-spells/m/mad-monkeys/)**:** This spell is….fucking funny, I’m NGL, you literally just drop a barrel of monkeys on the enemy, and now every round they have to save against Distraction (which is nauseated), hope they dont get their shit stolen, take damage, and generally just have he absolute worst time. Combine with a pit spell to create the ball pit of doom, and revel in the screeching madness.

## Utility

* [**Dispel Magic**](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/magic/all-spells/d/dispel-magic/) **(also Debuff):** Wow okay. So remember how I just described like a BUNCH of spells and how good they are? Crazy thing...the enemy can have spells too! Well we can’t have that. All jokes aside, dispel magic is a keystone to any casters build, as it lets you remove debuffs, weaken items, remove buffs, and all around smack anyone you don’t like with the no fun button. You WILL feel it if you don’t have this spell.
* [**Charm Monster**](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/magic/all-spells/c/charm-monster/)**:** Make anyone be your friend!!! This is great for handling situations diplomatically or just getting access to places that you definitely shouldn’t.
* [**Major Image**](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/magic/all-spells/m/major-image/) **(Also Control):** Create an audio, visual, smell and sensory illusion that everyone can see. Great for distractions, hiding, mind games, communications, just all forms of trickery and guile!
* [**Audiovisual Hallucination**](https://aonprd.com/SpellDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Audiovisual%20Hallucination)**:** So you cause a good number of people to hallucinate...basically anything you want. You can make them see any object, force or individual you can imagine or can designate them to imagine. This can...basically super fuck people as they see what you want them to see, causing them to run into traps, attack allies, like...the chaos this can cause is unimaginable and I FUCKING love it. This is frankly what I’ve always wanted for illusions. You want inspiration? Look up [Genjutsu](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=g_ysz6pKCnE&ab_channel=SekaiAMV) and just...have a fucking ball….ALSO reminder, its just a somatic component, so you can cast it even when silenced.
* [**Jester’s Jaunt**](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/magic/all-spells/j/jester-s-jaunt/)**:** Teleport a person touched 30 feet. Basically the same as the dimensional hop word and thats amazing. Sure, it gets a save an SR for enemies to be yeeted into poison pits which is hilarous but its much better to just position your allies in a way so that they don’t have to waste their movement! Fighters, eat your heart out!
* [**Shadow Enchantment**](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/magic/all-spells/s/shadow-enchantment/)**:** HAHAHAHAHAHA you can choose ANY enchantment spell from the Sorc OR psychic list...and the psychic actually gets some spells of this level early!!!! Shadow spells are amazing, giving us access to ENTIRE SUITES of spells. Sure you have to add a will save on top of it, but there are plenty of great spells that DON'T get saves or buffs you can cast that like...don’t need to be saved against.

## Protection

* [**Displacement**](https://aonprd.com/SpellDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Displacement)**:** Give yourself 50% miss chance on all attacks. It’s not better than mirror image, but does stack with it, and makes everything awful. Probably something to pick up later.

## 3rd Lvl Alt Sources

* [**Scrying**](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/magic/all-spells/s/scrying/) **(scroll):** Time to be the NSA and spy on people like an old school witch. Knowing things is always useful, but this isn’t something I’d expect you to cast every day.
