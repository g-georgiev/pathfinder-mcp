# 4th Level Spells

## Control

* [**Wall of Sound**](https://aonprd.com/SpellDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Wall%20of%20Sound): Like windwall but louder.

## Buff

* [**Dance of a Hundred Cuts**](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/magic/all-spells/d/dance-of-a-hundred-cuts/): Do you plan on doing melee fight stuff? Great! Take a hefty morale bonus that keeps going so long as you do the fight. Obviously, do not use it if you plan on casting good hope on everyone because they don’t stack, but if you want to turn yourself into a battle beast, this is a way to do it!
* [**Echolocation**](https://aonprd.com/SpellDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Echolocation) **(Also Utility):** OH hey blindsight! We don’t need this all of the time but it denies basically all illusory bullshit, so if you want to just skip all of the visual nonsense, here ya go! Ultimate see beyond sight!
* [**Freedom of movement**](https://aonprd.com/SpellDisplay.aspx?ItemName=freedom%20of%20movement): Anti-grapple. If you didnt pick up symphony of elysian hearts…which you should have…then you pick this up. But seriously…bardic masterpieces are amazing. You should just go pick up Elysian Hearts.
* [**Heroic Finale**](https://aonprd.com/SpellDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Heroic%20Finale) **(w/ quickened rod):** End your performance and immediately give another teammate a move or standard action. Okay when you are basically trading your standard action for a teammates standard action if they have something important planned. SIGNIFICANTLY better when quickened, so you are trading a swift action for a standard action.
* **✰[Virtuoso Performance](https://aonprd.com/SpellDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Virtuoso%20Performance)✰:** DUAL WIELDING PERFORMANCES!!! THIS IS WHAT WE ARE TALKING ABOUT! Now this is very expensive and we don’t want to be doing it all the time but if you can make the argument that bardic masterpieces can be affected by this? AHAHAAAAA WE ARE IN BUSINESSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSS.
* [**Army Across Time**](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/magic/all-spells/a/army-across-time/)**:** Read [This](https://www.reddit.com/r/Pathfinder_RPG/comments/d81sen/best_uses_for_allyarmy_across_time/) and have fun. Buy a ring of [Tactical Precision](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/magic-items/rings/ring-of-tactical-precision/) and take the [Battle Song of the People's Revolt](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/classes/core-classes/bard/bardic-masterpieces/masterpieces/battle-song-of-the-people-s-revolt-percussion-wind/) bardic masterpiece with the [Allied Spellcaster](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/feats/general-feats/allied-spellcaster-teamwork/) feat and the [fools for friends](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/traits/campaign-traits/second-darkness/fools-for-friends/) trait. Get a  Now throw your caster level through the roof and destroy the world because you are god I guess.

## Debuff

* [**Hold Monster**](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/magic/all-spells/h/hold-monster/)**:** Hold person, but now you can paralyze whoever! Remember, a paralyzed creature is helpless, so it can be coup-de-grace’d and hyper murdered at your leisure.
* [**Wandering Star Motes**](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/magic/all-spells/w/wandering-star-motes/)**:** Aka Dazing Dancing Dots, whose whole purpose is to flit around, bap people in the noggin, take away ALL of their actions for that round and then force them to keep saving while the team beats them up. Requires some teamwork to REALLY pull it off, but my god...is it funny when it works.

## Utility

* [**Dimension Door**](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/magic/all-spells/d/dimension-door/)**:** YAY TELEPORTATION. Teleport friends into kill range, get past the horde, get into the secret building, like its just a lot of options and we love that.
* **✰[Dominate Person](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/magic/all-spells/d/dominate-person/)✰:** Will Save (w/ SR). They fail? You now own this person. Yes that is a fucked sentence but thats basically it. You only have to spend a single round concentrating on the spell each day to deny them a save, you really just have to keep them from doing anything “obviously against their nature” or “incredibly harmful” which is a lot. Also someone can willingly fail a will save….so you might just be able to smack someone with charm person while they are mind fucked and have them forever!
* [**Shadow Conjuration**](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/magic/all-spells/s/shadow-conjuration/)**:** Jesus fuck. You can cast any summoning or creation conjuring spell of 3 level or lower...and that’s A LOT of spells. A lot of potentially REALLY good spells. Yes it requires an extra save, Yes, it’s not perfect but god damn does this give you versatility.
* [**Modify Memory**](https://aonprd.com/SpellDisplay.aspx?ItemName=modify%20memory): This requires a creative mind to take advantage of, but being able to permanently alter peoples memories is the kinda thing movie villains live for…and you can just have it as a spell. Nice.
* **✰[Complex Hallucination](https://aonprd.com/SpellDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Complex%20Hallucination)✰:** This is where I think Hallucination spells surpass image spells, because now you can include TACTILE elements to your hallucinations, having them be entirely immersed in the world you create for them. Now you can basically fool them into thinking anything and it looks, feels and sounds as real as their mind will allow, which is incredibly rude but also really really fun. Convince your enemy you’ve cast time stop and they can’t move as you slaughter their friends and family in the dream, only for them to wake up to the nightmare that is you. Enjoy being Aizen you sick fuck.

## Protection

* [**Path of Glory, Greater**](https://aonprd.com/SpellDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Path%20of%20Glory,%20Greater)**:** Fasting healing floor tiles! It basically gives the party fast healing 5 for the duration so long as they stand in the healing rift, which may not seem like much but trust me, it will come in handy especially since it is healing the ENTIRE paty.
* [**Greater Invisibility**](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/magic/all-spells/i/invisibility/) **(also Utility):** AH THE BLEEDING EDGE OF SCUMBAG CASTER TECHNOLOGY. Now, even if you use attacking spells, you are still invisible! What’s more, the rogue and all its cousins can add their damage dice without care and stealth becomes a breeze. You love it, you want it, and being an illusionist fucking rocks.

## 4th Lvl Alt Sources

* [**Break Enchantment**](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/magic/all-spells/b/break-enchantment/) **(scroll):** You don’t need to have this every day all the time but eventually someone is going to get hit by SOMETHING that needs this…so its better to have on you than not.
