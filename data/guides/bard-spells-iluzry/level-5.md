## Buff

* **✰[Vengeful Outrage](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/magic/all-spells/v/vengeful-outrage/)✰:** Point to your friend and fill with the power of friendship and anime while binding their soul to defeat Chaos…or whomever. Seriously its a massive buff, also for morale bonuses, and you’ll use it in almost every fight because there will be at least one guy you wanna capture or kill. Also you need to flex on barbarians by being able to give away their class features but better :)
* [**Music of the Spheres**](https://aonprd.com/SpellDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Music%20of%20the%20Spheres)**:** Mainly here for the healing and resistance, both of which are nice buuuut it should be noted very explicitly that EVERYONE gets it, even your enemies, so if you plan to be anywhere near melee, this isn’t for you.
* [**Unshakeable Zeal**](https://aonprd.com/SpellDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Unshakeable%20Zeal): Did you fail a thing? I’m sure you’ll do better next time. Hours per level duration is what really makes this worth it.

## Debuff

* [**Mydriatic Spontaneity, Mass**](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/magic/all-spells/m/mydriatic-spontaneity/)**:** Nauseated and blind but mass .Still a Will Save (w/ SR)
* [**Cloak of Dreams**](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/magic/all-spells/c/cloak-of-dreams/): Creatures who end up within 5 feet of your have to make a Will Save (w/ SR) or fall victim to your weed aura and go the fuck to sleep. This, is very funny, and basically can remove people from the fight for having the GALL to be near you.
* [**Mind Fog**](https://aonprd.com/SpellDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Mind%20Fog)**:** ...Damn...okay you drop a wave of like...okay look is like DMT. Will save (w/ SR), while in the fog. They fail???? ## Utility

* **✰[Bard’s Escape](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/magic/all-spells/b/bard-s-escape/)✰**: Greater Jester’s Jaunt. Team teleport! Get yourself in and out of trouble at a reasonable pace, and remember, this ONLY ends your turn.
* [**Dispel Magic, Greater**](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/magic/all-spells/d/dispel-magic/) **(also Debuff):** Absolutely rip magic apart. At this level? Everything has magic. Break their buffs. Unmagic magic weapons. Smack a whole group of annoying spells all at once. This is a groundbreaker spell, and you WILL be on your list of spells known
* **✰[Shadowbard](https://aonprd.com/SpellDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Shadowbard)✰:** YO FREE BARDIC PERFORMANCES THAT YOU DON'T HAVE TO DO THAT ALLOW YOU TO DO WHATEVER ELSE YOU WANT? THE BENEFITS OF *EXQUISITE ACCOMPANIMENT* AND *VIRTUOSO PERFORMANCE* IN ONE? THAT STACKS WITH BOTH??????? **THIS IS WHAT BARDS DREAM OF!!!!!! [Shadow Evocation](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/magic/all-spells/s/shadow-evocation/):** Access to EVERY evocation spell of 4th or lower. Remember this isn’t only blasting spells? This is control spells like Resilient sphere, and some buff spells and just...a whole mess of great great options. Amazing, spell, gives us a ridiculous amount of options, yes yes yes yes yes yes gold fucking star.
* [**Persistent Image**](https://aonprd.com/SpellDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Persistent%20Image)**:** No concentration image spell! Dozens of uses, no questions asked, can be really really creative.
* [**Suggestion, Mass**](https://aonprd.com/SpellDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Suggestion,%20Mass)**:** Everyone makes a Will Save (w/ SR) or you basically just win all social encounters. IDK what to tell you, its a really fun thing and works way better than the performance ever will.
* **✰[Scripted Hallucination](https://aonprd.com/SpellDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Scripted%20Hallucination)✰:** NO CONCENTRATION HALLUCINATION SPELL! This one is even better because it REACTS to what people do to make it even more real and again, you don’t need to know exactly what you want looks like or even exactly what to say. The spell lets their brain fill in the blanks of realism for you.
